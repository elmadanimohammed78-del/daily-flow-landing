How it works

Four keystrokes.
A full day tracked.

1

Type your task, press Enter

No setup, no categories to pick first. Just describe what you're starting. The timer begins the instant you hit Enter.

↵ Instant start
2

Switch tasks — it closes automatically

When you move to something new, just type it. Daily Flow timestamps the end of the previous task at that exact moment. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Auto close
3

Pause, edit, add notes anytime

Tap any task to rename it, change its category, or add context. Pause for calls and breaks — the clock only runs when you're actually working.

Full control
4

Export a professional report

End of day, tap Export. Get a polished PDF with Gantt chart, Excel spreadsheet, or HTML report — ready to send to clients or keep for records.
